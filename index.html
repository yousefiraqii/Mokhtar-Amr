AquaPure — Low-Cost Multi-Stage Water Filtration System

A 4-stage purification system that turns polluted canal water into safe drinking water using activated carbon from agricultural waste, electrocoagulation with recycled aluminum, natural coagulation with Moringa seeds, and UV-C sterilization. Built entirely from locally sourced and recycled materials to keep it affordable for rural households.

SABIL — Smart Automated Bot for Irrigation & Land Reclamation

An autonomous agricultural robot that performs plowing, seed dispensing, precision irrigation, and harvesting on a single solar-powered chassis. It uses soil moisture, gas, and environmental sensors with AI-based decision logic to apply the exact amount of water each plant needs and to assess land suitability before cultivation.

03
Green Energy — Hybrid Hydroelectric + Thermoelectric System

A dual-source renewable energy system that generates electricity from two inputs at once: a cross-flow turbine driven by flowing water, and Peltier modules that convert the temperature difference between sun-heated and water-cooled surfaces into power. The two outputs are combined and regulated into a battery.

E-Waste Absorbance Meter

A portable spectrophotometer built from discarded electronics that measures the concentration of a liquid sample by passing light through it and reading how much is absorbed. It applies the Beer-Lambert law to convert the light reading into concentration, with no need for a lab.

Smart Greenhouse Automation System

A closed-loop greenhouse that continuously monitors temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and light intensity, then automatically activates Peltier cooling, fans, humidifiers, drip irrigation, and LED lighting to keep conditions within optimal ranges. It runs autonomously and streams live readings to a phone via Bluetooth.
